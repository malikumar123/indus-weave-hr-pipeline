# CFO Findings — Indus Weave Textiles

Reporting period: April–September 2026.

## 1. Review the widening overtime gap in Faisalabad Stitching

D20 — Stitching, Faisalabad Plant — exceeded the 15% overtime
review threshold in five months, May through September.

Across those months, paid overtime exceeded observed overtime
by 2,915.5 hours. In September, paid overtime was 1,778.5 hours
against 748 observed hours: a difference of 1,030.5 hours,
or 137.77%.

Action:
Reconcile employee-level paid overtime with approvals and
biometric evidence, prioritizing September.

Limitation:
Each flagged month has 271–324 incomplete punch days.
Observed overtime may therefore be understated. The difference
does not establish overpayment.

Evidence: q05_overtime_comparison.csv and q06_overtime_excess.csv.

## 2. Review recurring payroll earnings after separation

The analysis identified 21 employee-month cases with positive
earnings in months after the employee's separation month.

For example, E10471 separated on 23 April 2026 but continued
to have monthly gross earnings of 132,000 from May through
September.

Action:
Check separation processing, payroll removal, and supporting
settlement or arrears documentation. Prioritize recurring
payments across multiple months.

Limitation:
Payroll month is available; actual payment dates are not.
Final settlements and arrears may explain some cases.

Evidence: q08_paid_after_separation.csv.

## 3. Review Annual leave approvals above entitlement

Twenty employees had approved eligible Annual leave days
during April–September exceeding their full-year entitlement.

The largest case shown had 28 approved days against a
14-day entitlement: 14 excess days, or 200% utilization.

Action:
Reconcile leave balances and documented exceptions, and add
an entitlement check to the approval process.

Limitation:
The supplied data does not establish whether additional
entitlement or carry-forward exceptions were authorized.

Evidence: q10_annual_leave_excess.csv.
